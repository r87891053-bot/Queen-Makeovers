About The Makeover

The Makeover – Beauty Salon & Academy is a beauty salon located in Kashipur, Samastipur, Bihar.

Services include bridal makeup, party makeup, facial, hair spa, hair cutting, hair treatment, manicure, pedicure, waxing, bleaching and mehndi design.

Beauty academy training is also available.
